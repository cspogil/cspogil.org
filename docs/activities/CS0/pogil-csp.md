# CS Principles (POGIL)

These activities were written for CS Principles with support from the {The POGIL Project}.
The author team included Tammy Pirmann, Brent Gray, Annie Giercyk, Jean Griffin, and Clif Kussmaul.

## Contents

- Creativity, Modeling & Simulation - Special Effects
- Digital Abstraction 1, Representing Information
- Digital Abstraction 2, Representing Text
- Digital Abstraction 3, Representing Images
- Digital Abstraction 4, Representing Instructions
- Digital Censorship
- Functions - Inputs & Outputs
- Hardware Abstraction 1, What is a Computer?
- Hardware Abstraction 2, Logic Gates
- Internet I - What is the Structure?
- Internet II - Where is the Data?
- Internet III - What is an Addresses?
- Number Systems 1, Decimal and Hex
- Number Systems 2, Binary
- Privacy in the Digital Age
- Search I - Text Search
- Search II - Web Search
- Texting & Social Media
- What are Algorithms?
